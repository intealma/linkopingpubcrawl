What is it?

A guided experience designed for teams, companies and groups looking for a fun way to get out of the office and spend time together.

We move between Linköping’s best bars, with one of our wonderful guides leading the group throughout the evening. This is not a traditional city tour; the guide keeps the group engaged with games, challenges, and team-building activities along the way.

Rules

How it works

01

Each venue offers one drink to participants

A welcome drink is offered at each pub during the crawl. Drinks at O'Learys are paid by guests themselves.

02

Keep the pace

About 45 min per stop. The group moves on together — no one is left behind.

03

20 years & ID

Age limit 20 years. Bring valid ID — required at all stops and entry to O'Learys.
